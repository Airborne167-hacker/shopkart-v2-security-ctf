About ShopKart

ShopKart started as a two-person side project and, one very long year later, is finally launching properly tomorrow morning. We picked a small catalogue of things we'd actually want on our own shelves — nothing more, nothing less.

We're a small team, which means the same three people wrote the code, tested the checkout, and are now refreshing this page at 1 AM double-checking everything is ready. If something looks unfinished, it's because it probably still is — we'd rather launch honestly than launch perfectly.

Questions before we open? Reach the team at hello@shopkart.example.
